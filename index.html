Stock Book

Daily stock take

Record each product's opening stock, stock received, sold quantity, wastage and actual physical closing count for the selected date. Tap Record to enter the physical closing count and save that product. Use Flag for explanation to mark an issue on this date only. Notes and flags from another date will not carry into this date. Flags are visual reminders only and never block recording, editing, sales or stock updates. Decimal quantities such as 0.5 and 0.25 are supported here.

Stock received, sales and stock corrections are included automatically. Calculated closing = opening + added − sold − wastage ± corrections. The calculated closing carries forward as the next recorded date's opening. Physical closing is kept separately to show any difference.
