Daily stock take

Record each product's opening stock, stock received, sold quantity, wastage and actual physical closing count for the selected date. Tap Record to enter the physical closing count and save that product. Use Flag for explanation to highlight and save an issue; flagged products cannot be recorded until you resolve the marker. Decimal quantities such as 0.5 and 0.25 are supported here.

Stock received and quantities sold are calculated automatically from saved stock-in and sales records. Expected closing = opening + added − sold − wastage. Saving reconciles live stock to physical closing when no later activity exists.
